How Correlation is Calculated
Per RiskPod: Each small matrix shows how the equity/rates/credit/FX/commodities exposure of each portfolio correlates to the same exposure in other portfolios. For example, the Equity matrix compares each portfolio's correlation to the S&P 500 anchor.
Total Correlation = Σ (RiskPod_Corr × Weight) Example: Alpha Growth vs Credit Opportunities = (Equity_Corr × 0.35) + (Rates_Corr × 0.20) + (Credit_Corr × 0.25) + (FX_Corr × 0.10) + (Comm_Corr × 0.05) + (Other_Corr × 0.05) = (0.48 × 0.35) + (0.52 × 0.20) + (0.68 × 0.25) + (0.18 × 0.10) + (0.18 × 0.05) + (0.15 × 0.05) = 0.168 + 0.104 + 0.170 + 0.018 + 0.009 + 0.008 = 0.42 (Total Correlation)
